# Nivesh Nyay Project Overview

## Concept

Nivesh Nyay is intended as an accessible digital platform for investor awareness and financial education.

## Product principles

1. **Clarity:** Explain financial terminology in plain language.
2. **Evidence:** Prefer authoritative sources and dated information.
3. **Transparency:** Clearly distinguish facts, assumptions, estimates and opinions.
4. **Safety:** Avoid presenting educational material as personalised investment advice.
5. **Accessibility:** Design for mobile and desktop users and avoid unnecessary complexity.

## Potential modules

### Education

Structured explanations of investing, risk, diversification, financial products and common terminology.

### Resource centre

A searchable collection of official documents, links and educational references.

### Decision-support layer

Future versions can provide calculators, comparison tools and scenario analysis while clearly showing assumptions and limitations.

### User experience

Responsive cards, navigation, search, filtering and concise explanations can make the platform useful to first-time investors.

## Future integrations

The architecture can later connect to regulated/authoritative data sources for market information, company disclosures, investor education material and other public datasets. Integrations should be isolated behind service adapters so providers can be replaced without rewriting the UI.
